Notable public [[digital garden|digital gardens]] and [[evergreen note]] repositories:

- [Andy Matuschak](https://notes.andymatuschak.org) — canonical reference for evergreen notes; sliding horizontal panes, dense bidirectional links, and proposition titles.
- [Maggie Appleton](https://maggieappleton.com/garden) — visual essays and design; popularized the seedling/budding/evergreen maturity model.
- [Gwern Branwen](https://gwern.net) — deep long-form research with link previews and citations across tech, stats, and psychology.
- [Tom MacWright](https://macwright.com/garden) — concise notes on software architecture, web development, and GIS.
- [Bryan Jenks](https://bryanjenks.dev) — tech-focused vault on CLI workflows, deep work systems, and PKM tooling.
- [Nick Milo / LYT Kit](https://notes.linkingyourthinking.com) — demo vault for the Linking Your Thinking framework and Maps of Content (MOCs).

### publishing tools
- **Obsidian Publish** — native hosting with interactive graph view and backlinks.
- **Quartz** — fast static site generator built specifically for Obsidian vaults.
- **Logseq / SilverBullet** — local-first outliners with built-in publishing.
