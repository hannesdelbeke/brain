# Scotland

Country in the north of the United Kingdom known for its mountainous terrain, lochs, coastal islands, and right-to-roam wild camping access.

Key regions and activities:
- **Highlands & Cairngorms:** Mountaineering, ridge walks, and multi-day treks.
- **Inverness & North Coast:** Kayaking and road trips ([[Kayak inverness]]).
- **Lochs:** [[Loch Lomond]], Loch Ness, wild camping and paddleboarding.
