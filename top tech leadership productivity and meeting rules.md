Core operating heuristics developed by leading tech founders to eliminate bureaucratic drag, maintain decision velocity, and protect creator focus.

| Leader / Thinker | Core Mechanism | Primary Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[[public/Elon Musk 6 rules for Tesla productivity|Elon Musk (Tesla)]]** | Walk out of non-value meetings; bypass chain of command; eliminate jargon and large gatherings. | Eliminate bureaucratic friction and restore direct communication. |
| **[[public/Jeff Bezos rules for meetings and decisions|Jeff Bezos (Amazon)]]** | 6-page narrative memos with silent reading; Type 1 vs Type 2 decisions; Two-pizza teams. | High-density thinking over charisma; rapid reversible decision velocity. |
| **[[public/Steve Jobs rules for meetings and focus|Steve Jobs (Apple)]]** | Directly Responsible Individual (DRI); ruthless meeting pruning; ban on slides. | Singular accountability, high debate engagement, and radical simplicity. |
| **[[public/Maker vs Manager Schedule|Paul Graham (YC)]]** | Protect 4-hour uninterrupted blocks for makers; cluster meetings at boundaries. | Prevent fragmented calendar sprawl from destroying deep engineering flow. |

## Common Cross-Cutting Themes
1. **Death of Large Meetings:** Every framework treats large meetings as an organizational failure mode.
2. **Writing Over Presentation:** Structured written artifacts (narrative memos, code, direct messages) beat slide decks and oral presentations.
3. **Decentralized Direct Ownership:** Small autonomous squads and single-owner DRIs over committee consensus.
4. **Protecting Deep Work:** Clear boundaries separating managerial coordination from creator deep work.
