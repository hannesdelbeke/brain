To convert full-resolution photos to compressed (Storage Saver) quality in [[Google Photos]]:
- go to http://www.photos.google.com
- Click `Settings` > `Manage storage` > `Convert existing photos and videos to Storage saver`

This does not affect pictures on [[Google Drive]]
