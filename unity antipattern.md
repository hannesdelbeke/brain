each [[Unity tool]] often adds a menu or submenu, resulting in a cluttered menu.

Often a tool could go under the existing menus like `Window` or `Tools` or `File`, to make it more unity native. Often devs like to add their tool or company name as a submenu, resulting in many menu entries, confusing the user.

IMO A better approach is to add [[tag|tags]] to your show tool command, so it can be found in [[Unity search]].
