> told [[Claude Code]] they have 1 million, how would it get rich in 20 years.

**Objective:** Maximise terminal real wealth at year 20 on a £1m portfolio (UK resident). No income requirement, with liquidity held in a separate cash buffer.

**Status:** Strategy research document, not regulated advice. Verify tax allowances annually as UK Budget rules change.

## Core Wealth Drivers

Over 20 years, terminal wealth is decided by five practical levers in order of certainty:

- **Tax wrappers (pension > ISA > gilts > GIA):** Adds £500k–£1m in sheltered compound growth (very high certainty).
- **Total cost control (0.20% vs 1.50% all-in):** Saves ~£950k in fee drag over 20 years (certain).
- **100% equity vs 60/40 balanced:** Adds £1m+ expected upside, with higher volatility (high expectation).
- **Behavioural discipline:** Avoiding panic selling during crashes protects against catastrophic downside (certain).
- **Factor tilts (small-cap value, quality):** Adds £0–£600k (low certainty).
- **Leverage at retail rates:** Roughly break-even after borrowing costs (low certainty).
- **Stock picking & active funds:** Negative expected value after fees (high certainty of drag).

Leverage and clever forecasting aren't the big levers. Wrappers, cost minimization, and staying invested drive 90% of the outcome.

### Baseline Arithmetic
Assuming £1m initial capital, zero further contributions, and a 0.20% all-in fee over 20 years:
- **5.0% nominal:** £2.65m terminal nominal (~£1.62m real at 2.5% inflation)
- **7.5% nominal (central case):** £4.25m terminal nominal (~£2.59m real)
- **10.0% nominal:** £6.73m terminal nominal (~£4.11m real)

A 1.0%/yr fee and tax drag costs roughly £750k of the central case.

## Wrapper Waterfall

Money flows down this sequence every tax year. Nothing enters a lower tier until the tier above is full.

**Tier 1: Pension (SIPP or Workplace)**
The highest return per pound in the UK. A higher-rate taxpayer contributes £1 gross for a 60p net cost. With 25% tax-free at drawdown, contributing at 40% relief and drawing at 20% delivers an instant ~42% risk-free uplift (55% uplift at 45% relief).
- Annual allowance is £60,000 gross (tapering to £10,000 for high earners above £260k).
- Up to ~£200,000 can go in year 1 via 3-year carry-forward, capped at your UK relevant earnings for that tax year.
- Lump sum tax-free allowance capped at £268,275. Funds locked until age 57 (from 2028).

**Tier 2: Stocks & Shares ISA**
£20,000/yr (£40,000 across a couple). Completely tax-free growth and withdrawals with zero reporting. Over 20 years, shifts £800,000+ of contributions into permanent shelter before growth.

**Tier 3: Junior Wrappers**
Junior ISA (£9,000/child/yr) and Junior SIPP (£2,880 net / £3,600 gross). Over a 60-year horizon, this is the highest-compounding pound in the entire plan.

**Tier 4: VCT / EIS (Only to Offset High Income Tax)**
30% income tax relief on up to £200k (VCT) or £1m (EIS). High risk, illiquid, and high fee. Treat strictly as a tax play and cap at 10% of total portfolio. Skip if there's no large income tax bill.

**Tier 5: General Investment Account (GIA)**
Holds the remaining balance in year 1 and gets drained into ISAs and SIPPs each April over two decades.

**Tier 6: Cash & Niche Shelters**
- **Low-coupon gilts:** Capital gains on UK gilts are CGT-exempt, delivering near tax-free yields for 45% taxpayers.
- **Premium Bonds:** £50,000 max buffer. Tax-free ~4% expected return with instant liquidity.

## Asset Allocation

100% growth equities with zero bond allocation. A 20-year horizon with no income needs doesn't require bond smoothing.

- **Global developed equity (70%):** Broad developed-market UCITS ETF (target OCF ≤ 0.15%).
- **Emerging markets (10%):** Broad EM IMI UCITS ETF (~0.18%).
- **Global small-cap (8%):** Global small-cap UCITS ETF (~0.35%).
- **Factor tilt (7%):** Small-cap value/profitability ETF (~0.40%).
- **Satellite sleeve (5% cap):** Optional allocation for high-conviction ideas (crypto, single stocks). Never topped up after losses.

*Alternative:* Replace sleeves 1–3 with a single FTSE All-World or MSCI ACWI IMI accumulating ETF if simplicity is preferred over 5 bps of optimization.

### Fund Selection Rules
- **Irish-domiciled accumulating UCITS:** Cuts US dividend withholding tax from 30% to 15% via the US-Ireland treaty, saving ~0.20%/yr.
- **UK reporting status mandatory:** Prevents gains from being taxed as offshore income at 45% instead of 24% CGT.
- **No currency hedging:** Hedging equity back to GBP over 20 years creates drag without long-term return benefit.
- **Flat-fee platforms:** Use fixed-fee or fee-capped platforms. Uncapped 0.45% platform fees cost ~£180,000 on £1m over 20 years.

## Deployment Schedule

**Year 1, Day 1**
1. Move 12 months of living expenses into Premium Bonds outside the portfolio.
2. Fill personal and spousal ISAs (£40,000 total).
3. Maximize pension contributions via carry-forward up to UK relevant earnings.
4. Deploy the entire remaining balance into the GIA as a lump sum. (Lump sum beats dollar-cost averaging two-thirds of the time; if risk tolerance demands it, split into max 4 monthly tranches).

**Annual Ritual (Every 6 April)**
1. **Bed & ISA:** Sell £20k+ from GIA, repurchase inside ISA (and spouse ISA).
2. **Bed & SIPP:** Sell from GIA, contribute to pension up to annual allowance.
3. **Harvest CGT allowance:** Realize gains up to annual exempt threshold (£3,000) to step up base cost.
4. **Harvest losses:** Bank any losses in down years to offset future gains.
5. **Rebalance on ±5% drift:** Rebalance using fresh contributions first, selling only within tax-sheltered accounts.

## Standing Investment Rules

- Never sell during a market drawdown except to harvest tax losses or rebalance. Expect two or three 40% drops over 20 years.
- No market timing or holding dry powder waiting for headlines to clear.
- Satellite sleeve capped strictly at 5% and never topped up after a fall.
- Total ongoing cost must stay under 0.25%/yr all-in.
- Review once a year on 6 April. Checking balances constantly turns a 20-year horizon into a stressful daily trade.
- Fund lifestyle needs from earned income, never by chipping away at portfolio capital.

## Higher-Aggression Options Assessed

Options evaluated for extra growth, sorted by viability:

**Starting or buying an operating business**
The highest expected-value use of £1m and the only realistic path to a 10x+ return. It requires active operational execution rather than passive management.
🟢 **Verdict: Highest expected return (active).** Ring-fence venture capital while running the passive wrapper plan on core funds. Note that UK Business Property Relief is capped at £1m from April 2026.

**Modest leverage (1.2–1.3x)**
Kelly-optimal leverage at 5.5% retail borrowing rates is ~1.0x (unfavorable). Only becomes compelling if borrowing costs drop below 4.5%.
🟡 **Verdict: Marginal.** If used, cap at 1.25x on cheap margin outside ISAs/SIPPs with strict deleveraging triggers. Small gain, real tail risk.

**Concentrated stock picking**
Most individual stocks underperform index benchmarks, and active selection carries negative expected alpha after costs.
🟡 **Verdict: Confine to 5% satellite sleeve.** Treat as conviction experiments, never core capital.

**Crypto**
Extreme volatility with zero underlying cash flows and full GIA tax liability.
🟡 **Verdict: Confine to 5% satellite sleeve.** Position size so a complete loss doesn't affect terminal goals.

**Buy-to-let property**
UK residential property is heavily penalized: Section 24 mortgage interest non-deductibility, 5% stamp duty surcharge, EPC rules, void risk, and illiquidity.
🔴 **Verdict: Unviable without local edge.** Underperforms global index funds after taxes and management friction.

**Private equity / venture capital**
Retail-accessible PE funds lag public equities after heavy management and carry fees. Top-tier access requires institutional scale.
🔴 **Verdict: No.** Confine unlisted bets strictly to tax-advantaged EIS/VCT routes.

**Leveraged ETFs (2x/3x daily)**
Daily reset mechanics cause severe volatility drag in choppy markets.
🔴 **Verdict: Excluded.** Structurally broken for multi-year holding.

## Tax and Estate Planning

**Capital Gains Tax (CGT)**
18% basic / 24% higher rate above the £3,000 allowance. Keep draining assets into ISAs and SIPPs to eliminate ongoing exposure.

**Dividends**
£500 tax-free allowance, then 8.75% / 33.75% / 39.35%. Accumulating funds in a GIA still generate notional taxable dividends (excess reportable income).

**Inheritance Tax (IHT)**
Nil-rate band £325,000 plus £175,000 residence band, then 40%. From April 2027, unused pension pots are brought into the taxable estate. Business Property Relief is capped at £1m from April 2026 (50% relief above).

**Non-Residency & Expatriation**
Leaving the UK requires 5 full tax years of non-residence to avoid CGT clawback on assets realized abroad.

## What Failure Looks Like

All catastrophic failure modes are self-inflicted rather than market events:
- Paying 1.5%/yr in adviser and platform fees (loses 25% of terminal wealth).
- Leaving £1m in a GIA for 20 years instead of systematically migrating to wrappers.
- Panic-selling during a 35% drawdown and buying back after the rebound.
- Buying non-reporting offshore funds and paying 45% income tax on capital gains.
- Allowing speculative satellite holdings to creep beyond 5% of portfolio value.

## Executive Summary

1. Hold 12 months of spending in Premium Bonds outside the portfolio.
2. Maximize pension carry-forward up to relevant earnings, then personal and spousal ISAs.
3. Lump-sum the remaining balance into an unhedged, Irish-domiciled global equity index at <0.25% cost.
4. Run the annual 6 April ritual: bed & ISA, bed & SIPP, harvest CGT allowances and losses.
5. Cap satellite bets at 5% and ignore market noise for 20 years.
6. Central expectation: ~£4.25m nominal (~£2.59m real in today's money).
