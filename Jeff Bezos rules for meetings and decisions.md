Jeff Bezos established several foundational operating mechanisms at Amazon to counter corporate stagnation, preserve startup velocity, and eliminate performative meeting culture.

## 1. The 6-Page Narrative Memo (Ban on PowerPoint)
PowerPoint presentations are banned for internal executive reviews.
- **Narrative Format:** Proposals are written as structured 6-page memos with real sentences and arguments rather than bullet points.
- **Silent Reading Period:** Meetings begin with 20–30 minutes of silent reading so everyone ingests the full context before speaking.
- **Why It Works:** Humans read roughly 3x faster than a presenter speaks. Narrative writing forces the author to think through logic and trade-offs rather than hiding gaps behind bullet points and charisma.

## 2. Type 1 vs. Type 2 Decisions
Decision velocity depends on recognizing reversibility:
- **Type 1 (One-Way Doors):** Irreversible, high-consequence decisions (e.g. major acquisitions, architectural lock-in). Must be made slowly, deliberately, with exhaustive consultation.
- **Type 2 (Two-Way Doors):** Reversible, iterative decisions. Must be made quickly by high-judgment individuals or small teams.
- **The Failure Mode:** Large companies mistakenly treat all Type 2 decisions like Type 1, creating bureaucracy and decision paralysis.
- **70% Rule:** Make Type 2 decisions with ~70% of the information you wish you had. Waiting for 90%+ is too slow.

## 3. The Two-Pizza Team
Keep autonomous teams small enough to be fed by two pizzas (typically 6–10 people).
- Caps communication channels and coordination overhead.
- Grants small squads end-to-end ownership of distinct services.

## 4. Disagree and Commit
Do not waste cycles seeking unanimous consensus or social harmony.
- Team members are expected to voice authentic disagreements and debate vigorously.
- Once a decision is made, everyone commits 100% to execution without second-guessing.
