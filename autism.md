Autism is characterized by distinct cognitive wiring and perceptual processing styles rather than purely behavioral deficits.

### Monotropism
Allocating maximum processing power to a single active interest at a time. When locked into a technical architecture, mechanic, or project, background sensory data and secondary tasks get actively filtered out.

### Bottom-Up Processing
Predictive processing differences where the brain processes raw sensory and logical details first before assembling the generalized model. Enables exceptional edge-case detection and deep pattern recognition, but increases sensory and cognitive load since raw inputs aren't automatically compressed.

### Cognitive Inertia & Task Switching
Directly tied to monotropism. An active cognitive tunnel takes substantial metabolic energy to start, stop, or redirect. Unexpected context switches feel jarring because the mental state model has to be rebuilt from scratch.

### Interoception
Delayed recognition of internal bodily signals (hunger, thirst, bladder pressure, physical exhaustion, temperature). Early cues often go unnoticed during deep focus until reaching an acute threshold.

### Double Empathy Problem
Communication friction isn't a one-way deficit, but a bidirectional mismatch between different neurotypes. Autistic-to-autistic communication is typically direct and high-bandwidth. Autistic-to-neurotypical mismatches occur because neurotypicals rely on unstated subtext, tone calibration, and phatic signals, while autistic communication prioritizes literal accuracy and informational density.

### Hyper-Systemizing
The drive to analyze rules, structures, variables, and feedback loops to make systems predictable and optimizable (code, taxonomies, tools, workflows, games).

### Algorithmic Masking & Camouflaging
Manually calculating eye contact, tone modulation, and conversational scripts using conscious executive function rather than subconscious instinct. High cognitive overhead often leads to post-social fatigue or burnout.

### Intimacy via Info-Dumping & Parallel Play
Connection built through deep domain sharing (info-dumping) or low-demand co-presence (parallel play / working on separate tasks in the same space) rather than phatic small talk.
