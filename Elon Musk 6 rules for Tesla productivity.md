Leaked 2018 internal email sent by Elon Musk to Tesla employees outlining six operating rules to eliminate corporate drag, bureaucratic theater, and bloated meetings.

## 1. Avoid Large Meetings
Excessive meetings are the blight of large companies and almost always get worse over time.
- Get rid of large meetings unless certain they provide value to everyone present.
- If necessary, keep them extremely short.

## 2. Leave if You Are Not Adding Value
Walk out of a meeting or drop off a call the moment it is clear you are not adding or receiving value.
- It is not rude to leave; it is rude to force someone to stay and waste their time.

## 3. Drop Frequent Meetings
Ditch recurring or frequent meetings unless dealing with an urgent crisis.
- Meeting cadence should drop sharply once the emergency is resolved.
- Solve issues asynchronously via chat, direct messages, or offline collaboration.

## 4. Bypass the Chain of Command
Communication must travel the shortest direct path between people doing the work, not through managerial hierarchies.
- Forcing communication through managers slows execution and distorts information.
- Anyone can talk directly to anyone across departments to solve a problem.

## 5. Be Clear, Not Clever (No Acronyms or Jargon)
Avoid acronyms, buzzwords, and technical jargon for tools, parts, or processes.
- Jargon requires mental translation, creates barriers for new hires, and inhibits clear thinking.
- Choose plain words that need no explanation.

## 6. Pick Common Sense Over Company Rules
If following a company rule is ridiculous in a given context, ignore or change the rule.
- Rules are heuristics, not laws; never let policy override practical judgment.
