> Provenance is the origin, history, and trail of ownership of an object, data, or code. It provides the historical context of how something came to be.

> [!example]
> In software and data, tracking provenance involves recording:
> - authorship (who wrote or generated it, e.g. human vs AI)
> - timestamps (when it was originally created or modified)
> - source (where the data was extracted or copied from)
> - transformations (what changes were applied over time)
