
#### 1. **Apple iPad Pro (M5, 2025)**

- **Performance:** Apple’s M5 chip delivers laptop‑class speed, excellent for multitasking and creative apps.
- **Portability:** Ultra‑thin, lightweight, and small screen options (11" or 13").
- **Battery:** 10–12 hours typical use.
- **USB‑C / Thunderbolt:** Supports **USB4/Thunderbolt 3**, enabling fast external drives and docking.
- **Stylus:** Apple Pencil Pro with haptic feedback and tilt sensitivity — excellent for drawing.
- **Best for:** Creative professionals who want portability and stylus precision.

#### 2. **Microsoft Surface Pro 10 (2025)**

- **Performance:** Runs full Windows 11 with Intel Core Ultra processors; handles heavy workloads.
- **Portability:** Detachable keyboard, thin client design, 13" display.
- **Battery:** Around 12–15 hours depending on workload.
- **USB‑C / Thunderbolt:** Supports **USB4/Thunderbolt docking** for external GPUs, drives, and monitors.
- **Stylus:** Surface Slim Pen 2 with tactile feedback.
- **Best for:** Users needing Windows apps, coding, or productivity with pen input.

#### 3. **Lenovo Yoga 9i Slim 2‑in‑1**

- **Performance:** Intel Core Ultra or AMD Ryzen options; strong multitasking.
- Portability: Convertible design, thin and light.
- **Battery:** 12+ hours with efficient processors.
- [[USB-C]] / [[Thunderbolt]]: Thunderbolt 4 ports for docking and fast external SSDs.
- **Stylus:** Integrated pen with good drawing support.
- **Best for:** Balanced use — productivity, entertainment, and creative sketching.

---

### ⚡ Why These Fit Your Criteria

- **Performance:** All three avoid the slowdown issue common in budget laptops.
- **Portability:** Thin, light, and small‑screen options make them easy to carry.
- **Battery:** Each offers **10–15 hours**, enough for all‑day use.
- **USB‑C Charging:** Standard across all models for convenient docking.
- **Thunderbolt/USB4:** Ensures **fast external drives and docking stations**.
- **Stylus Support:** Each has advanced pen input for drawing or note‑taking.

---

### 🛒 Shopping Notes

- **Apple iPad Pro M5 (11" or 13")** — premium pricing, best stylus experience.
- **Surface Pro 10** — versatile, Windows ecosystem, strong docking.
- **Lenovo Yoga 9i Slim** — slightly more affordable, excellent balance of features.

If you want **maximum portability with stylus focus**, go iPad Pro.  
If you want **Windows productivity with docking**, go Surface Pro.  
If you want a **hybrid laptop‑tablet with strong battery and Thunderbolt**, go Lenovo Yoga.
