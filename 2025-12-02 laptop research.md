Early lightweight laptop and 2-in-1 research exploring thin tablet and hybrid form factors with stylus and Thunderbolt support.

## Candidate Overview

**Apple iPad Pro (M5, 2025)**
- **Performance:** Apple Silicon M-series chip with fast mobile multitasking.
- **Portability:** Ultra-thin and lightweight (11" or 13").
- **Battery:** 10–12 hours typical runtime.
- **Connectivity:** USB4 / Thunderbolt 3 port for fast external SSDs.
- **Stylus:** Apple Pencil Pro with haptic feedback and tilt sensitivity.
- **Best for:** Tablet-first sketching and lightweight mobile editing.

**Microsoft Surface Pro 10 (2025)**
- **Performance:** Full Windows 11 on Intel Core Ultra; handles standard desktop toolchains.
- **Portability:** Detachable keyboard 2-in-1 form factor with 13" display.
- **Battery:** 12–15 hours depending on workflow.
- **Connectivity:** Dual Thunderbolt 4 / USB4 ports for external monitors and eGPU docking.
- **Stylus:** Surface Slim Pen 2 with tactile haptics.
- **Best for:** Windows productivity, desktop IDEs, and pen note-taking.

**Lenovo Yoga 9i Slim 2-in-1**
- **Performance:** Intel Core Ultra or AMD Ryzen with strong multi-threading.
- **Portability:** 360-degree convertible chassis.
- **Battery:** 12+ hours with modern efficiency cores.
- **Connectivity:** Dual [[Thunderbolt]] 4 / [[USB-C]] ports for docking.
- **Stylus:** Integrated garaged stylus.
- **Best for:** Balanced daily productivity, coding, and sketching.

## Criteria Summary

- **Thermals & speed:** Core Ultra / M-series chips avoid low-end throttling.
- **Portability:** Compact sub-1.4 kg chassis across all three candidates.
- **Battery life:** 10–15 hours real-world runtime.
- **Docking:** USB-C PD and Thunderbolt standard across all models.
- **Stylus support:** Low-latency active pens for sketching and notes.

## Recommendation

- For **tablet-first portability and drawing:** iPad Pro.
- For **desktop Windows workflows with pen:** Surface Pro.
- For **clamshell coding with 360-degree stylus capability:** Lenovo Yoga.
