How to use an [[AI agent|AI agent]] as an active Socratic sparring partner to test your comprehension, expose knowledge gaps, and practice [[the Feynman technique]].

### Prompt Templates

**Explain-and-Grade**
> I am going to explain [[topic]] to you in simple terms as if you're a beginner. Grade my explanation, identify any logical gaps or glossed-over steps, and challenge any unproven assumptions.

**The Curious 12-Year-Old (Socratic Sparring)**
> Act as a curious 12-year-old who knows nothing about this topic. I will explain how [[topic]] works. Stop me and ask 'why?' or 'how does that work?' every time I use jargon, buzzwords, or skip an underlying mechanism.

**Misconception Trap Generator**
> I am studying [[topic]]. Generate 3 tricky multiple-choice questions with subtle, tempting misconception traps to test if I truly understand how the mechanism works versus just memorizing the terms.

**Analogy Stress-Tester**
> Here is an analogy I came up with to explain [[topic]]: "[Insert your analogy]". Tell me where this analogy breaks down and what misconceptions it might accidentally create.

### The Practice Loop
1. **Draft your explanation:** Write 1–2 paragraphs explaining a concept from memory without looking at notes.
2. **Submit to AI:** Use one of the prompt templates above.
3. **Handle the pushback:** When the AI flags jargon or asks a probing question, answer it in plain words without looking up the solution.
4. **Re-study the stall:** If you can't answer the AI's question, you've found your exact knowledge gap. Go back to your source material, review that specific part, and update your note.
