[[Obsidian]]
[[Uniform Resource Identifier|URI]]

`(obsidian://vault/other_vault/note)` will open the note `note` in the vault `other_vault`.`

also see the [Advanced URI](https://github.com/Vinzent03/obsidian-advanced-uri) plugin
