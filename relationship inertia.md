**Relationship inertia** describes the psychological and logistical phenomenon where couples transition into major milestones (moving in, buying property, marriage) through gradual accumulation of constraints rather than active, deliberate choices.

Pioneered by psychologists Scott Stanley, Galena Rhoades, and Howard Markman (*Family Relations*, 2006, [DOI: 10.1111/j.1741-3729.2006.00418.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1741-3729.2006.00418.x)).

---

## Sliding vs. Deciding

* **Sliding:** Transitions happen incrementally to solve immediate logistical or financial needs (*"it's cheaper to split rent"*, *"we've been together this long, buying makes sense"*).
* **Deciding:** Explicit, mutual discussions where commitment is chosen based on deep compatibility and shared values before structural entanglement occurs.

---

## The Inertia Mechanism

```
Sliding Cohabitation ──► Accumulated Constraints ──► High Exit Penalty ──► Trapped by Inertia
(Immediate convenience)  (Leases, deeds, pets)      (Logistical barrier)   (Holding pattern)
```

1. **Premature Entanglement:** Cohabitation introduces shared leases, joint furniture, pets, and entwined finances before core long-term compatibility is verified.
2. **Escalating Exit Costs:** The logistical, financial, and emotional difficulty of separating increases with each adult milestone.
3. **The Holding Pattern:** Couples stay together not because relationship quality or attraction is high, but because breaking up feels overwhelmingly disruptive.
