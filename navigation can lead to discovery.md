Efficient navigating through links, doesn't let the user discover new concepts.
Use navigation in a way where it can lead to [[fun discoveries]].

> [!example]
> A site that sells chairs, links all chair pages to a central chair page.
> Users who are only looking for a chair, will never discover matching tables, or other furniture they can have in their living room.

Disagree, seems bad example, better would be user finds perfect chair, then on chair page it shows matching tables etc. Ikea does this.

With organic [[public/link|linking]] the user discover things they didn't knew they wanted.
