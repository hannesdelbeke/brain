Knowing whether a note was written by a human or generated by an AI agent drastically alters how to evaluate and trust its contents.

### Why Provenance Matters
- **Human-authored text:** Carries real-world experience, idiosyncratic perspective, and personal conviction, but may reflect cognitive bias, emotional skew, or outdated assumptions.
- **AI-generated text:** Offers comprehensive structural breadth and fast synthesis, but risks subtle hallucinations, confident errors, and superficial fluency without [[skin in the game]].

Preserving this context across vault edits and migrations ensures future readers (and AI retrieval agents) know what standard of scrutiny to apply.
