melatonin is a [[hormone]] that helps with [[sleep]].
recommended for adults over age 55, or adults with sleeping issues.

don't take it 
- have liver or kidney problems
- have [[rheumatoid arthritis]], multiple sclerosis or [[lupus]], or any other autoimmune condition. because it stimulates the immune system, which can trigger or worsen disease flares
